Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026
This policy describes what the CodeBreaker app itself does today. It is a plain-language template, not legal advice — have it reviewed before relying on it for an App Store / Play Store / Steam submission.

Summary

CodeBreaker is a single-player word puzzle game. It does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not transmit your gameplay data to any server by default.

What's stored, and where

Your progress (round reached, correct guesses, shard balance, unlocked achievements, and settings like audio/theme) is saved locally on your device only:

  • Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux): a JSON file in your OS user-data folder (e.g. %APPDATA%/CodeBreaker on Windows).
  • Web / mobile: your browser or app's local storage on-device.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself. You can erase it at any time via Settings → "Reset All Progress", or by uninstalling the app.

Analytics

The app includes an analytics module (for understanding play patterns and balancing difficulty) that is disabled by default. If a future build enables it, events are anonymous gameplay counters (e.g. "round completed") with no personal identifiers, and this policy will be updated to name the destination before that ships.

Purchases

The in-app store in the current build runs in a local sandbox — no payment details are collected or transmitted by CodeBreaker itself. When distributed through Steam, the Apple App Store, or Google Play, real purchases are handled entirely by those platforms under their own privacy policies; CodeBreaker only receives confirmation that an entitlement was granted.

Third parties

No advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs are currently integrated. If any are added in the future (e.g. an ad network on mobile), this policy will be updated first to disclose what they collect.

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is collected, CodeBreaker does not knowingly collect data from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
